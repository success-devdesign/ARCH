Frequently asked questions

Questions? We've Got Answers.

What services does ARCH Ecommerce Solution offer?

Shopify store design, Shopify development, conversion rate optimisation, Shopify SEO, product and store setup, and ongoing store maintenance and support.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Our focus is Shopify, and that is where we do our best work. If you are unsure whether Shopify is right for you, ask us on a consultation.

Can you redesign my existing Shopify store?

Yes. We review your current store, keep what works and redesign what does not, so the result suits your brand and your customers.

Can you build a Shopify store from scratch?

Yes. We handle the structure, design, development, product setup and launch.

Do you offer custom Shopify development?

Yes. That includes theme customisation, custom functionality and tailored store experiences.

Can you improve my store's conversion rate?

We can improve the things that influence it: product pages, navigation, user journeys, speed and checkout. We do not promise a specific increase, because results depend on your products, traffic and market.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with Shopify store owners remotely, whichever country they are in.

How long does a Shopify project take?

It depends on the scope. A focused setup takes less time than a full redesign with custom development. After a discovery conversation we give you a clear timeline.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Store maintenance and support covers updates, troubleshooting, improvements and technical assistance after launch.

Measurement

The seven numbers we report on.

Revenue is not profit. A growth plan has to track product costs, margin, ad spend, fulfillment, refunds and discounts alongside these.

Conversion rate
The percentage of store visitors who complete a purchase.
Average order value
Average revenue per order (AOV).
Customer acquisition cost
The cost to acquire a new customer (CAC).
Customer lifetime value
The estimated value a customer generates over the relationship (LTV).
Return on ad spend
Attributed revenue divided by advertising spend (ROAS). It is not the same as profit.
Contribution margin
The revenue remaining after relevant variable costs.
Repeat purchase rate
The share of customers who purchase more than once.
